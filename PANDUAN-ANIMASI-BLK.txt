- 3D spotlight tilt pada kartu modul.
- Ripple effect tombol.
- Animated counter modul/file/catatan.
- Animasi modal dan gate password.
- Shimmer tombol dan ribbon scanner.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Efek berat otomatis dikurangi di mobile.
